Club28 Badminton Club is one of the clubs in the Pennsylvania-Delaware area. I
visited the club in their Philadelphia location. It is spacious with comfortable
ceiling height for clear and lift. The club is 20 minutes outside the central
city area. It is situated in a quiet neighborhood with not much going on.

I played there on a Saturday but the club is fairly empty. It's possible that
not many players are there during new year holiday. However, out of 11 courts,
only 4 courts are occupied from 6pm. The club has 6 courts in the front and
another 5 courts in the back. In the front, there are 2 courts that have lower
ceiling heights than others. Additionally, the club has ample space for walking
around and watching others play. The spacing between courts can be tight as
birds often fly into each other's court. The club also has a small weight room,
which I believe is reserved for players who train there. The light in the club
is dim so it is not too bright. However, on certain shots, it is difficult to
track the bird. The courts are covered in badminton mattress, which absorb the
tension from jumping nicely.

It costs around $18-$20 for 2-hour play, which I think is a great deal.


Club website: https://www.club28badminton.com/philadelphia

# Final Score

Light: 6/10.

Floor: 7/10.

Vibe:  6/10.

Cost:  8/10.
